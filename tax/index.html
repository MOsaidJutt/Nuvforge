Questions

Frequently asked.

Is NuvTax part of FBR?

No. NuvTax by Nuvforge is an independent tax support service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Federal Board of Revenue.

Is every return PKR 3,500?

No. PKR 3,500 is the starting fee for straightforward salaried return filing. More complex cases may require a separate quote.

Why do you need my June 30 balance and asset information?

Individual filing may require both an Income Tax Return and Wealth Statement. The information needed depends on your tax position and filing requirements.

Who prepares my return?

Your return is reviewed and prepared under the guidance of senior tax professionals with around three decades of experience working within Pakistan's tax system, including senior careers in federal tax administration.

Can you teach me how to file my own return?

Yes. Hands-on filing training is available for people who want to understand the IRIS workflow and become more confident managing their own tax filing.

Can I send all my documents over WhatsApp?

We recommend using the secure document-sharing process provided after your initial qualification instead of placing sensitive financial records in a public form.
