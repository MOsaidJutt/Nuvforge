About NuvTax

An independent tax support service built on the idea that you should understand what is filed in your name.

Who we are

NuvTax is the tax practice of Nuvforge, a software and services company. We provide income tax return filing, NTN registration, wealth statement preparation, return review and hands-on IRIS training for individuals and businesses in Pakistan.

The experience behind it

Behind NuvTax is a team with around three decades of experience inside Pakistan's tax system, including senior careers in federal tax administration. That background shapes how we work: we have seen what happens when returns are filed carelessly, and we know which details actually matter when a return is examined later.

To be explicit about it, that experience does not make us part of FBR and gives us no special access or influence. It gives us judgement about how the system works in practice, which is a different and more honest thing to offer.

How we work

Most filing services optimise for volume. Documents arrive, a return is submitted, an invoice is issued, and the client never really learns what was declared in their name. We think that is the wrong model, because the person whose name is on the return is the person who carries the consequences.

Our process is built around four steps: review what you actually have, understand what it means for your position, approve it yourself, then file. The explanation step is not an optional extra; it is the point.

Who we work with

  • Salaried professionals filing straightforward annual returns
  • First-time filers who have never registered
  • Freelancers, remote workers and IT exporters receiving foreign payments
  • Small business owners and sole proprietors
  • People with several unfiled years who want to get straight
  • Pakistanis living abroad with tax obligations at home

What we will tell you honestly

If filing does not apply to you, we will say so. If your case is more complex than a fixed price covers, we will quote it properly rather than discovering it halfway through. If training will not be enough for your situation, we will tell you that instead of selling you a session.
