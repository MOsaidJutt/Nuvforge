Income Tax Return Filing in Pakistan

We review your income, prepare your return, explain what is being declared, and support you through submission. Starting from PKR 3,500.

Who needs to file an income tax return?

In Pakistan, filing requirements depend on your income, your assets and your circumstances rather than on job title alone. People who commonly need to file include salaried employees above the taxable threshold, freelancers and consultants, business owners and sole proprietors, people who own property or vehicles, and anyone who wants to appear on the Active Taxpayers List.

If you are unsure whether filing applies to you, that is the first thing we check. It takes one WhatsApp message and costs nothing.

When should you file?

The standard due date for individual and AOP income tax returns is 30 September following the end of the tax year. Filing early is materially easier: you have time to locate missing documents, ask questions and correct problems rather than rushing in the final week.

Please verify current figures. Tax rates, thresholds, penalties and due dates are set by the Federal Board of Revenue and change with each Finance Act. Always confirm the current position for your tax year on the official FBR website or with us before acting.
