Learn to File Your Own Return

Hands-on IRIS training for people who would rather understand the system than depend on someone else every year.

Who this is for

Training suits people who want to stop outsourcing something they would rather understand. That includes salaried professionals filing simple returns each year, freelancers who want control over their own numbers, small business owners, finance and accounts staff who need practical IRIS familiarity, and people who simply dislike handing over financial information without knowing what happens to it.

What a session covers

  • How IRIS is structured and where things actually live in the portal
  • Registration and profile basics
  • Selecting the correct return for your situation
  • Entering salary, tax deducted and other income sources
  • Completing the wealth statement and understanding reconciliation
  • Common mistakes and how to spot them before submitting
  • What to do after filing and which records to keep

How it runs

Sessions are practical and worked through on the portal rather than delivered as a lecture. Where possible we use your own real situation, because filing a hypothetical return teaches far less than filing yours. Sessions can be one-to-one or arranged for a small group or team.

An honest note. Training makes you capable of handling straightforward filing yourself. If your situation is genuinely complex, with multiple income sources, business accounts or years of unreconciled history, we will tell you that plainly rather than sell you a session that will not be enough.

Pricing

Training is quoted based on scope and whether it is individual or group. Message us with what you want to be able to do by the end and we will tell you what it takes.
