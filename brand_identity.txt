Brand Architecture

Nuvforge is the master brand — the forge.
Products and solutions that come out of it use the "Nuv" prefix (e.g. NuvTax). Nuv-products inherit the Nuvforge palette and typography and may reuse the NF mark; they do not get unrelated visual identities. Client-facing white-label deployments carry the CLIENT's brand, not ours.

Color Palette

Deep Navy
#0F172A

Primary brand color. Use for headlines, navigation, backgrounds, premium corporate applications — and as TEXT ON TEAL BUTTONS.

Nuvforge Teal
#14B8A6

Primary accent color. Use for button fills, icons, highlights, and technology elements. NOT for text on light backgrounds and NOT paired with white text (see Accessibility Rules).

Dark Teal
#0F766E

Text-safe teal. Use for links, teal-colored text, and labels on light backgrounds (Off White / white).

Slate Gray
#64748B

Secondary text and supporting information.

Off White
#F8FAFC

Primary light background.

Silver
#C5CED8

Borders, dividers, subtle interface elements, and secondary graphics.

Accessibility Rules (WCAG AA)

1. Buttons: Teal (#14B8A6) fill with Deep Navy (#0F172A) text — 7.2:1 contrast, passes AA. Never white text on teal (2.5:1, fails).
2. Links & teal text on light backgrounds: use Dark Teal (#0F766E), not Nuvforge Teal (#14B8A6 on Off White is 2.4:1, fails).
3. On Deep Navy backgrounds, Nuvforge Teal (#14B8A6) is fine for text and accents (7.2:1).

Recommended Color Combination

Deep Navy + Teal + Off White

That should become the most recognizable Nuvforge visual combination.
